# Code of Conduct

Ephemera adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Ephemera to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, please tell the maintainers privately at
**[CONDUCT CONTACT EMAIL]**. Every report will be reviewed and handled promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code
of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful. Enforcement follows the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.
